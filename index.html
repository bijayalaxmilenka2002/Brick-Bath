The Turnkey Blueprint

How We Deliver Stress-Free Excellence

From 3D blueprint to white-glove handover, our standardized 5-step engineering process ensures precision, zero hidden costs, and total peace of mind.

01

Free Consultation & 3D Blueprint

Our senior bathroom architect visits your home, measures the layout, assesses plumbing/drainage, and creates a photo-realistic 3D render.

02

Fixed Quote & Material Selection

Zero surprises or sudden cost escalations. You receive an itemized, fixed contract covering branded tiles, CP fittings, and labor.

03

Dust-Sealed Demolition & Waterproofing

We dust-barrier the area, carefully strip old fixtures, install leak barriers, and perform mandatory 48-hour ponding/flood tests.

04

Precision Tiling & Fixture Assembly

Certified artisans lay laser-aligned tiles, install concealed diverters, vanity cabinetry, glass enclosures, and ambient cove lighting.

05

32-Point Audit & White-Glove Handover

Water pressure, drainage angles, and silicone seals are rigorously inspected before deep-cleaning the space and issuing your written 5-year warranty card.

Every hour of our 21-day turnkey transformation is scheduled to eliminate delays and ensure pristine craftsmanship.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our turnkey bathroom renovation process, warranties, timelines, and payment milestones.

What services does BricknBath provide?

BricknBath provides comprehensive turnkey bathroom transformation solutions, including: in-home design consultation & 3D visualization, complete dust-controlled demolition, advanced multi-layer polymer waterproofing, plumbing and electrical modifications, large-format tile laying, sanitaryware and CP fittings installation, custom vanity cabinetry, toughened glass shower enclosures, and final white-glove quality inspection.

What is BricknBath?

BricknBath is a specialized, luxury bathroom transformation brand powered by BricknBar. We transform outdated, leaking, or inefficient bathrooms into modern, functional, and luxurious private sanctuaries through professionally managed, fixed-price renovation packages.

How do I book a bathroom renovation consultation?

Booking a consultation is seamless: you can call us directly at +91 72058 89111 or Toll-Free at 1800-212-0151, submit your details using the form on this website, or connect via WhatsApp. Our design directors will contact you within 2 hours to discuss your project and schedule an in-home site assessment.

Which cities do you currently serve?

We currently execute turnkey bathroom transformations across Bhubaneswar, Cuttack, Puri, and adjoining areas in Odisha. For luxury projects located elsewhere, please reach out to our customer concierge to check feasibility.

How long does a complete bathroom renovation take?

Our standard turnkey packages (Aura and Prestige) are executed in 21 to 25 working days. Bespoke luxury suites (Elite and Signature) requiring imported sintered stone or custom glass cubicles take approximately 25 to 35 days. We provide a binding handover date in your agreement.

Do you provide a warranty on waterproofing and workmanship?

Yes! Every project includes a 5 to 10 year written warranty on our dual-coat polymer waterproofing barrier and plumbing craftsmanship. In addition, all branded fittings (Jaquar, Kohler, Grohe) come with their official manufacturer warranties of up to 10 years.

Are there any hidden costs after the project begins?

No. Fixed-price transparency is the bedrock of our company. After our initial site measurement and 3D plan approval, we deliver a comprehensive itemized quote covering every tile, fitting, pipe, waterproof layer, and labor expense. What you sign is what you pay.

Can I choose my own tiles, sanitaryware, and vanity designs?

Absolutely. You can select from our curated catalogue or visit our flagship Experience Center in Jayadev Vihar, Bhubaneswar, where our designers will assist you in selecting tiles, vanity wood finishes, faucets, and lighting live.

Can we live in our house while the renovation is underway?

Yes! Our team enforces clean-site residential protocols. We install zipped dust barriers across the bathroom doorway, lay protective floor runners across passageways, bag debris daily, and adhere to strict quiet hours so your daily home life is undisturbed.

Who handles demolition and debris disposal?

BricknBath manages 100% of demolition, bagging, carting, and municipal debris disposal. Homeowners do not have to coordinate with any external labor or transport vehicles.

Do I get to see what my bathroom will look like beforehand?

Yes! Our 3D visualizers generate realistic renders showing your exact chosen tiles, vanity placement, mirror backlight, and shower enclosure. On-site work only commences once you have reviewed and signed off on the 3D design.

What are your payment milestones?

Payments are linked strictly to observable physical progress stages (e.g., Booking & 3D approval, Demolition & Waterproofing stage, Tile completion, and Final Quality Handover). You inspect each milestone before releasing payments.
